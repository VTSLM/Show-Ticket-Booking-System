# Code of Conduct

TicketFlow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, give and accept feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project and the team, and do not harass or demean anyone in issues, reviews, comments or chats.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to a maintainer (@VTSLM or @https-hari) or to the course instructors. Reports are handled confidentially.
